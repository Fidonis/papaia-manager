    Recent notifications

    Good to know

    • Each event is reported once per occurrence: a failed job, a backup that turns overdue, a service that turns unhealthy, a certificate and its expiry date, a release or add-on version. The manager remembers what it reported, so a restart does not send it again.
    • A service that was stopped on purpose counts as stopped. While a job, a restore, an upgrade or a stack action is running, services are not checked.
    • An overdue backup needs a backup schedule. Add-on updates are compared with the catalogs on disk, so they show up after a catalog refresh.
    • A webhook receives a JSON post with event, severity, message, timestamp and details. Its URL is never shown again after it is saved, and redirects are not followed.
    • Notifications carry no job log and no error text, only what is needed to find the job.
