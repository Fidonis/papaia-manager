A job refers to a credential by name. The ingester reads it from ai/rag/.env (needs a restart after a change) or from the encrypted secrets.yaml kept here, which it reads when it needs the value, so a new credential works at once.

The encryption key is in the same .env and a backup contains both files. That keeps a credential out of casual sight (a copy of the file, a screenshot, a log); it does not protect it from someone who can read both.
