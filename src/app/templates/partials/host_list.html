Rows follow the module rows on the Services page: state rides on a left edge stripe, healthy takes the neutral border so the stripe routes attention to the exceptions, and the dot repeats what the colour says for print and for a colour-blind reader. The rows carry their own `border-t` rather than sitting in a `divide-y` container: `divide-*` sets the colour of every side of every row after the first, and wins on specificity over the stripe.
