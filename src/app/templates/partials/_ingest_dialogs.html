Delete ?

The job is removed from jobs.yaml and stops running. What it already embedded stays in unless you remove it too.

Its runs are deleted too. They and their logs cannot be restored.

This ingester cannot delete runs, so the runs of the job stay in its history.

This job belongs to the Embedding page and is created again with the next embedding. An upload that is still waiting keeps its files until the time limit.

Delete runs of

Deletes the history of a job, with the log of every run, for the days you pick. This cannot be undone. What the job embedded is not touched, and a run that is still working is kept.
